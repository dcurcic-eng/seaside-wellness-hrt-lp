Who is a good candidate for HRT?+

Women in perimenopause or menopause and men with symptoms of low testosterone are often evaluated. Whether HRT is right for you depends on your symptoms, history and lab results, which we review together at your consultation.

What happens at the $249 consultation?+

Krista reviews your symptoms and medical history, completes a personalized hormone evaluation, recommends the right lab work and talks through your treatment options. You leave with a clear next step.

Do I need lab work before starting?+

Yes. Lab work shows where your hormone levels are, so any plan is based on your results. We recommend the right labs at your consultation and review every result with you. Labs are billed separately.

Is HRT covered by insurance?+

Our HRT visits are offered at the clear prices shown on this page. Coverage for labs and medications depends on your plan. Call us at 850-446-8099 and we will help you understand your options before your visit.

Can I do follow-up visits by telehealth?+

Yes. After your consultation, follow-up visits can be done in our Largo office or by telehealth, whichever suits you. Follow-ups are $99 per visit, or included with the Hormone Wellness Membership.

Do you treat both women and men?+

Yes. We evaluate women in perimenopause and menopause and men with symptoms of low testosterone. Every plan is built around your own symptoms, history and labs.
